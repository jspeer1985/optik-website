## 💡 Risk Factors & Mitigation

### Revenue Risks

**Risk 1: Slower Merchant Adoption**
- **Mitigation**: Aggressive referral program, free trials, white-glove onboarding
- **Impact**: Revenue delayed 6-12 months

**Risk 2: Lower GMV per Merchant**
- **Mitigation**: Focus on high-volume verticals (restaurants, retail)
- **Impact**: 20-30% revenue reduction

**Risk 3: Fee Compression**
- **Mitigation**: Diversify revenue streams, add premium features
- **Impact**: 15-25% margin pressure

**Risk 4: Token Price Volatility**
- **Mitigation**: Treasury diversification, stablecoin reserves
- **Impact**: Variable, managed through hedging

### Market Risks

**Risk 5: Solana Network Issues**
- **Mitigation**: Multi-chain expansion plan
- **Impact**: Temporary growth pause

**Risk 6: Regulatory Changes**
- **Mitigation**: Legal compliance, jurisdictional flexibility
- **Impact**: Geographic restrictions

**Risk 7: Competition**
- **Mitigation**: First-mover advantage, network effects
- **Impact**: Market share dilution
