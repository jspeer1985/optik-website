## Utility Features

### 1. Platform Fee Reduction

Merchants staking OPTIK receive reduced transaction fees:

| OPTIK Staked | Platform Fee | Monthly Savings* |
|--------------|--------------|------------------|
| 0            | 2.5%         | $0               |
| 10,000       | 2.0%         | $500             |
| 100,000      | 1.5%         | $1,000           |
| 1,000,000    | 0.75%        | $1,750           |

*Based on $100,000 monthly revenue

**Annual Savings Example:**
- Merchant with $1.2M annual revenue
- Stakes 1M OPTIK tokens
- Saves **$21,000/year** in platform fees
- ROI: 210% assuming $0.01 token price

### 2. Staking Rewards (12-20% APY)

Stakers earn rewards from:
- Platform transaction fees (70% distributed to stakers)
- NFT minting fees
- Royalty distribution pool

**APY Tiers:**

| Stake Amount | Lock Period | APY   |
|--------------|-------------|-------|
| 10,000+      | None        | 12%   |
| 100,000+     | 3 months    | 15%   |
| 500,000+     | 6 months    | 18%   |
| 1,000,000+   | 12 months   | 20%   |

### 3. Governance Rights

OPTIK holders can vote on:
- Platform fee adjustments
- Treasury allocation
- Feature prioritization
- Merchant grant approvals
- Protocol upgrades

**Voting Power:**
- 1 OPTIK = 1 vote
- Minimum 100,000 OPTIK to create proposals
- Quadratic voting to prevent whale dominance

### 4. NFT Minting Discounts

Pay NFT minting fees with OPTIK:
- **Standard**: 0.5 SOL per NFT
- **With OPTIK**: 0.3 SOL + equivalent OPTIK

## Deflationary Mechanisms

### 1. Buyback & Burn
- 10% of platform revenue used to buy OPTIK from market
- Tokens burned quarterly
- Transparent on-chain burns
- Target: 50% supply burned over 10 years

### 2. NFT Royalty Burns
- 1% of all NFT royalties paid in OPTIK
- Automatically burned on collection
- Estimated 5M OPTIK/year at scale

### 3. Fee Burning
- 0.1% of every platform transaction
- Immediate on-chain burn
- Estimated 2M OPTIK/year at scale

**Total Annual Deflation:** ~7M OPTIK (0.7% of supply)
